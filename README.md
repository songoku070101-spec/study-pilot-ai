# StudyPilot AI – Smart Study Planner

StudyPilot AI is a modern AI-powered study planner designed to help students organize their study schedule, manage tasks, and stay productive.

The application helps students plan subjects, track study tasks with deadlines, monitor progress, and maintain study consistency using smart suggestions and productivity tools.

---

## Features

- Add and manage study subjects
- Create study tasks with deadlines
- Mark tasks as completed
- Smart study recommendation system
- Study progress tracking with progress bar
- Weekly study progress chart
- Exam countdown tracker
- Study streak system
- Pomodoro focus timer
- Motivation messages based on productivity
- Clean and responsive modern UI
- Light and dark mode support

---

## Problem It Solves

Students often struggle to organize multiple subjects, assignments, and exam deadlines. StudyPilot AI helps solve this problem by providing a centralized productivity tool that helps students prioritize their study tasks, track progress, and stay consistent with their study routine.

---

## How It Works

1. Add your study subjects.
2. Create tasks with deadlines.
3. Track progress on the dashboard.
4. View exam countdowns and study streaks.
5. Use the focus timer to stay productive.
6. Follow AI-powered recommendations to decide what to study next.
